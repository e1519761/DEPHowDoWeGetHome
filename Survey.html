    ADD YOUR WALK

    Your walk home
    is data.

    This project is all about your stories. We designed it as an open-source, community map where everyone can share their real everyday experiences. Ushahidi works like a neighborhood noticeboard—you can pin your own walk home, explore other people’s journeys, and see what makes getting home easy or confusing. Everyone’s voice counts equally here.

    Sharing your experience relies on trust, so keeping your privacy safe is our top priority. Here are our core promises to you:

    No names, no home addresses

    We focus on the route, not your front door. No identifying details are ever shown.

    No hidden agendas

    We are upfront about what data we collect and why we need it.

    You’re in control

    We only collect what is necessary, and you can leave any optional questions blank.

    We’re here to learn from your lived experience, so share as much or as little as you feel comfortable with!

    02 / DATA MANAGEMENT

    From your story
    to shared knowledge.

    Every contribution helps us understand how residents experience their everyday journeys. To make sure the information is useful and responsibly shared, each submission follows a clear process.

    Entries are reviewed by our team before they appear on the Map of Realities. We may contact contributors when clarification or changes are needed.

    Once published, the information contributes to a growing picture of how people navigate Singapore's HDB estates. Published entries are also exported regularly to update our research findings.

    Users and roles

    Administrator Team lead

    Sets the overall survey design and outreach direction; oversees the Editors and Partner relations.

    Editors The team

    Review, code and publish entries to the website; update the consolidated map.

    Contributors You, the public

    Submit entries but cannot publish them. Contributors vary in age and background.

    Partners External parties and institutions

    View and download the data for further research and projects.

    03 / FORM FEEDBACK

    Listening, learning
    and improving.

    The Ushahidi form is designed to make participation accessible and straightforward, allowing residents to share their everyday experiences of finding their way home.

    Feedback helps us understand how people experience the submission process, including which questions are clear, which steps may be confusing, and where the form could become easier to use.

    These observations help us refine the way information is collected, making participation more inclusive and improving the quality of contributions.

    04 / PARTICIPATE

    Ways to
    take part.

    There is no single way to share your experience. Whether you have a few minutes or want to walk with us, every contribution helps us understand the journey home.

    2 MIN

    Drop a cue

    One spot on your way home, one thing you notice there, one photo.

    8 MIN

    Map your walk

    Your whole route from the stop or station, with the spaces and senses along it.

    30–45 MIN

    Walk with us

    We walk your route with you and record it, so you can just talk. Sessions in Queenstown, Holland Village, Jurong East and Bishan. Email us to join.

    Your everyday walk could help make someone else's journey home easier.
